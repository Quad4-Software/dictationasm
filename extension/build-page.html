Offline dictation with on-device Moonshine. Version __VERSION__.

Chrome / Chromium

Download the CRX and drag it onto chrome://extensions with Developer mode enabled. Or unpack the ZIP and Load unpacked.

Download CRX Download ZIP

Firefox

Temporary install: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, choose Load Temporary Add-on, and pick the XPI. For permanent install, use a Mozilla-signed build from a release when available.

Download XPI

Updates

Packed installs can auto-check /build/updates.xml (Chrome) and /build/updates.json (Firefox). In the extension, open Settings to point at a fork or self-hosted site and use Check for updates.

After install

  1. Open the extension popup and click Connect bridge tab.
  2. Keep the bridge tab open while translating, speaking, or recording.
  3. Allow microphone access on the bridge tab for dictationasm.
